Professional standards record

Professional Standards & Complaints Pathways

A documentary record of the direct professional clarification pathways concerning Lal Lal Wind Farm acoustic work, the separate capacities in which professional-body complaints were made, and the governing AAS and AAAC processes.

Publication boundary

Complaint material is not reproduced

This page does not publish the formal professional-body complaints, submissions made within those complaint processes, investigation material, or confidential complaint-process correspondence. It publishes only independently existing correspondence and documents that preceded, or exist separately from, the confidential professional-body processes.

The lodging of a complaint is not a finding. Nothing on this page should be read as establishing professional misconduct, unsatisfactory performance, breach of a code, or other wrongdoing. Any outcome will be recorded only if and to the extent that the relevant professional body makes a determination capable of public disclosure.

Capacity matters

Two distinct complainant pathways

The record deliberately separates complaints made by the Beneficiary in private capacity from complaints made by the Corporate Trustee.

Beneficiary — private capacity

Australian Acoustical Society (AAS)

The AAS Constitution permits a complaint to be made by any person concerning specified professional-conduct or performance matters. The AAS complaints recorded here were made independently by the Beneficiary in his private capacity.

Where later Trustee correspondence was supplied to the Beneficiary, it was supplied for information and independent consideration. The Trustee did not become the complainant by providing that material.

Corporate Trustee — Trustee capacity

Association of Australasian Acoustical Consultants (AAAC)

The AAAC procedure provides a pathway for an external person or party to raise a complaint against an AAAC member firm after direct attempts at resolution. The AAAC complaints recorded here were made by LEWIS TROJAN HORSE PTY LTD as Trustee of the Owen Robert Inter Vivos Trust.

The AAAC process is treated as confidential in accordance with its published procedure. Only the existence and broad procedural status of the complaints are recorded here.

Australian Acoustical Society

AAS — Beneficiary private-capacity pathway

The public record below is limited to the independent pre-complaint correspondence and the factual existence of the complaint pathway.

2 August 2026Beneficiary — private capacity

Gustaf Reuterswärd — AAS complaint pathway

The Beneficiary had an earlier direct correspondence record with Mr Reuterswärd dating from January 2024 concerning aspects of SLR's Lal Lal Wind Farm acoustic reporting, including K15aa, Special Audible Characteristics, J14aa, report versions and the question of professional independence.

Separately, in 2026 the Trustee issued a formal procedural-fairness Notice to Mr Reuterswärd on 26 July, provided subsequent documentary updates for information, and issued a Completion of Procedural Opportunity Notice on 11 August. Those Trustee documents expressly formed part of the Trustee's own administrative record and did not themselves make a professional-misconduct finding.

The Beneficiary subsequently advised the Trustee that he had independently lodged an AAS complaint in his private capacity. The complaint itself is not published on this website.

29 August 2026Beneficiary — private capacity

Thomas Evans — AAS complaint pathway

The Trustee corresponded directly with Resonate Consultants and Mr Evans between 5 and 21 August 2026 concerning the scope, assumptions and professional basis of Resonate's 30 September 2024 peer review. The correspondence records Resonate's response and the Trustee's subsequent requests for clarification.

After completing that direct correspondence pathway, the Trustee provided the relevant correspondence and documentary material to the Beneficiary for information and independent consideration. The Trustee did not direct, authorise or request that the Beneficiary make a complaint. The Beneficiary's subsequent AAS complaint was made independently in his private capacity; the Trustee was not the complainant.

The complaint itself is not published on this website.

AAS professional standards pathway

The AAS Constitution provides that a complaint may be made to Federal Council by any person in relation to the categories identified in clause 17.2. The Society's Code of Ethics addresses responsibility, competence, objective and truthful professional statements, conflicts and professional reputation.

Public professional statement

SLR Public Information Session — Gustaf Reuterswärd on K15aa

The public documentary record also includes an information-session statement by Gustaf Reuterswärd addressing the K15aa operational noise-limit pathway. The presentation records that the endorsed NCTP did not include a noise limit for K15aa and that SLR subsequently monitored K15aa and derived a limit.

Why it is published here: The excerpt is independently existing public material and is not confidential professional-body complaint material. It is relevant because it records the professional's own public explanation of a matter that also appears in the K15aa/NCTP documentary record. Publication of the statement is not a finding by AAS, AAAC or this website concerning professional conduct.

How to read this record

Correspondence first; findings only if formally made

The public record allows readers to examine the questions put directly to the relevant professionals and firms before the professional-body pathways were invoked. It does not ask readers to treat the existence of a complaint as proof of its allegations.

Published

Pre-complaint correspondence, procedural-fairness notices, professional-body constitutions, codes and published complaints procedures.

Not published

Formal AAS or AAAC complaint documents, complaint-process submissions, confidential correspondence, committee material or other non-public complaint-process records.
