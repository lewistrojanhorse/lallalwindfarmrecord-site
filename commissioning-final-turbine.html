Commissioning of the Final Turbine

A plain-English reconciliation of the planning-permit, endorsed-plan and monitoring obligations that follow from the final-turbine commissioning milestone, together with the presently disclosed date record.

Why this date matters

One milestone, several mandatory pathways

The commissioning of the final turbine is not merely a project-history date. The Planning Permit and endorsed management material use that milestone to start, end or measure a number of administrative obligations. Other endorsed material uses closely related wording such as “commencement of operation”. This page preserves the exact source wording while showing how the requirements fit together.

This page does not determine technical compliance. It identifies the trigger, the requirement, the disclosed documentary record and any question that remains open.

Governing documentary pathway

How the NCTP obtains authority within the permit framework

Planning Permit PL-SP/05/0461 is the authorising document for the Lal Lal Wind Farm. Condition 25 requires a Noise Compliance Testing Plan and provides that, once approved and endorsed by the Minister for Planning, the NCTP forms part of the Planning Permit.

Regulation 131B of the Environment Protection Regulations identifies the authorising document as the source that determines the relevant wind farm noise standard, including any conditions that modify or replace requirements of NZS 6808:2010. Within that statutory framework, the endorsed NCTP derives its authority through the Planning Permit and establishes the project-specific noise-testing pathway.

Planning PermitAuthorising document
Condition 25Requires and incorporates the endorsed NCTP
Regulation 131BRecognises the standard specified by the authorising document
Endorsed NCTPProject-specific monitoring and reporting pathway

The documents do not use one uniform expression. The Planning Permit refers to commissioning of the facility and full operation of the facility, while the endorsed Noise Compliance Testing Plan (NCTP) establishes the project-specific operational noise pathway using commissioning of the last turbine as the trigger for pre-operation requirements, post-construction noise monitoring, compliance testing and reporting. Once endorsed, the NCTP forms part of the Planning Permit under Condition 25 and therefore sits within the same permit framework as Conditions 12 and 17, which also use, or operate by reference to, commissioning of the last turbine. This page preserves those differences in terminology while demonstrating how the operational noise pathway aligns with the same commissioning milestone reflected elsewhere in the Planning Permit, rather than treating the various expressions as automatically synonymous.

Planning Permit triggers

Public commissioning chronology

The following contemporaneous public records preserve the developing commissioning chronology from December 2019 through June 2020. The landscaping records identify an implied final-turbine milestone, while the March 2020 minutes record that the post-construction noise monitoring program was delayed because the wind farm was not operating at full capacity.

Community Reference Group chronology — post-construction noise monitoring

Following the 4 December 2019 Community Reference Group minutes, which linked the landscaping program to commissioning of the last turbine, subsequent Community Reference Group minutes preserved a contemporaneous administrative record concerning commencement of the post-construction noise monitoring program.

24 March 2020The post-construction noise monitoring program was delayed in commencing because Lal Lal Wind Farms was not operating at full capacity, with AEMO limiting the amount of generation.
12 May 2020The post-construction noise monitoring program remained delayed because Lal Lal Wind Farms was not operating at full capacity. Operating levels recorded were 102 MW at Yendon at night, 42 MW during the day and 5 MW at Elaine, compared with total project capacity of 228 MW.
8 September 2020The post-construction noise monitoring program remained delayed because Lal Lal Wind Farms was not operating at full capacity.

Other commissioning-linked monitoring record

Other documentary material records how the final-turbine commissioning milestone, and the related concept of commencement of operation, were used in administering monitoring and reporting pathways.

Operational noise framework and the K15aa pathway

Read in sequence, the endorsed NCTP records the applicable background-noise pathway and pre-operation requirement, the Jacobs review independently records that relationship, and SLR's later correspondence states how it regarded the K15aa baseline as having been established.

Commencement-of-operation record

The documentary record contains separate descriptions of background sound, limited turbine operation during the 2019 K15aa monitoring program and commencement of operation. These records are presented together for documentary reconciliation and are not treated here as determining technical compliance.

NZS 6808:2010 documentary reference

The Appendix H chronology is to be read together with the NZS 6808:2010 definition of background sound:

“Background sound — The A-frequency-weighted L90 centile level measured prior to the installation of any wind turbines in an area.”

Together, the records preserve three distinct concepts: the Standard's definition of background sound, EPA's administrative position on commencement of operation, and SLR's recorded chronology of turbine operation and generation during the 2019 K15aa monitoring program.

Earlier FOI records — K15aa background report

EPA FOI-100-2025. In the Beneficiary's historical EPA FOI request, the Lal Lal Wind Farm Compliance Baseline Monitoring Report 640.11872-R01 v1.1 dated February 2021 was expressly requested. EPA's decision identified nine responsive documents, but the scheduled documents did not include that foundational K15aa report, and the decision did not separately address whether the report was held or why it was not provided.

DTP FOI CS015467. The Beneficiary also requested the date on which the SLR Lal Lal Wind Farm Compliance Baseline Noise Monitoring Report 640.11872-R01 dated February 2021 was submitted to the Department, Minister or Responsible Authority. In its decision dated 1 October 2024, the Department of Transport and Planning advised that extensive searches located no information relevant to the request and that it appeared no relevant documents had been submitted to the Department.

NCTP post-construction monitoring stages

The NCTP uses commissioning of the final turbine as the trigger for both mandatory post-construction monitoring stages. The disclosed Elaine and Yendon completion dates are preserved separately.

NCTP section 6.0 reporting chronology

Section 6.0 starts the reporting period when the monitoring undertaken for the relevant compliance stage is completed. The corresponding compliance report was required to be submitted to the Minister for Planning within six weeks after that monitoring was completed.

Conflicting commissioning-date record

The operator's later response to EPA preserves both its stated completion-of-commissioning date and its shortened restatement of the endorsed NCTP monitoring trigger. The two source extracts are reproduced below. The exact endorsed wording is preserved in the NCTP exhibits and source document linked from this page.

Public landscaping chronology

The CRG minutes and Community Bulletin imply a final-turbine commissioning milestone of 18–19 November 2019.

Later operator statement

In its 14 April 2025 response to EPA Information Gathering Notice IGN-00008382, Lal Lal Wind Farms stated that the wind farm completed commissioning on 10 December 2020 and commenced operating from that date. The same response separately identified earlier generation dates for the Yendon and Elaine sections.

Why the distinction is preserved

Construction completion, energisation, generation, individual turbine commissioning, final-turbine commissioning and commencement of operation are not automatically the same event.

Administrative reconciliation

Trustee enquiry seeking the commissioning completion record

After identifying the later operator statement that the wind farm completed commissioning on 10 December 2020 and commenced operating from that date, the Trustee asked Lal Lal Wind Farm for the commissioning certificate or equivalent commissioning-completion document for the final turbine. The enquiry expressly stated that it was made for administrative record purposes only and that no allegation was made.

Follow-up and documentary status. After no acknowledgement had been received, the Trustee sent a follow-up dated 4 March 2026 seeking confirmation that the enquiry had been received and recorded in the Enquiries and Complaints Register in accordance with the published Community Engagement Policy and Complaints and Enquiries Procedure.

See also: Commissioning Enquiry - Trustee correspondence concerning the asserted commissioning-completion date and request for documentary confirmation.

How to read the date record

The disclosed sources do not use one uniform date

The present record includes public material implying final-turbine commissioning on 18–19 November 2019, later administrative closure dates of 18–19 November 2020, a later agency statement identifying 10 December 2020, and separate descriptions of electricity export, turbine generation and operation. The repository preserves each source and the purpose for which its date was used rather than substituting an undefined expression or treating one statement as silently resolving the others.
