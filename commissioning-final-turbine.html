Commissioning of the Final Turbine

A plain-English reconciliation of the planning-permit, endorsed-plan and monitoring obligations that follow from the final-turbine commissioning milestone, together with the presently disclosed date record.

Why this date matters

One milestone, several mandatory pathways

The commissioning of the final turbine is not merely a project-history date. The Planning Permit and endorsed management material use that milestone to start, end or measure a number of administrative obligations. Other endorsed material uses closely related wording such as “commencement of operation”. This page preserves the exact source wording while showing how the requirements fit together.

This page does not determine technical compliance. It identifies the trigger, the requirement, the disclosed documentary record and any question that remains open.

Public landscaping chronology

The December 2019 CRG minutes and June 2020 Community Bulletin both publicly connected the landscaping closure date with the final-turbine commissioning milestone.

Later reported commissioning date

A later agency record states that the last turbine was commissioned and the wind farm commenced operating on 10 December 2020. The repository attributes that date to its source rather than silently treating it as resolving every other date reference.

Why the distinction is preserved

Construction completion, energisation, generation, individual turbine commissioning, final-turbine commissioning and administrative closure are not automatically the same event.

How to read the date record

The disclosed sources do not use one perfectly uniform date

The present record includes a public landscaping chronology that implies final-turbine commissioning on 18–19 November 2019, later administrative closure dates of 18–19 November 2020, a later agency response identifying 10 December 2020, and other commissioning-related descriptions. The repository therefore preserves each source, identifies the purpose for which the date was used, and avoids substituting “fully operational” or another undefined expression for the actual source wording.
