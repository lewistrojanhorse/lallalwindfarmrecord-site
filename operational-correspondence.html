Operational Correspondence

Selected correspondence and contemporaneous source material concerning commissioning, the K15aa updated background report, operational noise observations and later Trustee enquiries.

Capacity and chronology

Beneficiary record and continuing Trustee administration

The earlier correspondence was created by the Beneficiary before establishment of the Owen Robert Inter Vivos Trust. Following establishment of the Trust, the Trustee incorporated that material into the continuing administrative and evidentiary record. The original capacity and chronology are preserved.

4–7 October 2024

Final-turbine commissioning enquiry

The Beneficiary asked what authority required notification of turbine commissioning dates or requested the final commissioning dates for the Yendon and Elaine sections. The operator stated that its notifications and reports were completed in accordance with planning requirements and that it was not obliged to provide the requested information.

21–31 July 2026

Crystallised Administrative Position — procedural opportunity completed

On 21 July 2026, the Trustee issued the Notice — Crystallised Administrative Position to the Lal Lal Wind Farm Operator. That Notice afforded the Operator a reasonable opportunity to identify any additional documentary material, authorising document or documentary explanation relevant to the twelve matters identified in Schedule 1.

On 31 July 2026, the Trustee issued a further Notice — Completion of Procedural Opportunity. That Notice records that the procedural opportunity previously afforded had concluded and that, as at its date, no substantive response had been received identifying additional documentary material, an authorising document or a documentary explanation relevant to the matters raised.

The Trustee records that it will proceed upon the basis of the documentary record as presently reconciled. The completion Notice does not expand the earlier Notice. Should relevant documentary material later be identified, the Trustee remains willing to consider it as part of the continuing administrative and evidentiary record.

26 July - 8 August 2026

Professional acoustic record - opportunity to clarify

On 26 July 2026, the Trustee issued a Notice to Mr Gustaf Reuterswärd of SLR Consulting Australia providing an opportunity, as a matter of procedural fairness, to clarify the documentary and authorising basis for professional positions, methodologies, interpretations, statements and conclusions forming part of the Lal Lal Wind Farm administrative record.

The Notice addressed, among other matters, the authorising basis relied upon, NZS 6808:2010, the approved Noise Compliance Testing Plan, the K15aa background-noise dataset, representative monitoring, operational compliance testing, Special Audible Characteristics, daylight-saving time and statements concerning what occurred during monitoring and assessment activities.

26 July 2026

Notice issued

The Trustee afforded a direct opportunity to identify clarification, additional documentary material or an authorising basis not presently apparent within the Trustee's record. The Notice expressly stated that it was not an allegation of wrongdoing or professional misconduct and did not record a concluded finding.

3 August 2026

Response period concluded

The Notice invited a response by 3 August 2026 and allowed for additional time to be requested if reasonably required.

8 August 2026

No response recorded

As at 8 August 2026, the Trustee records that no response, acknowledgement or request for additional time has been received in response to the Notice.

No adverse inference is drawn merely from the absence of a response. The record therefore remains without any clarification or additional material from Mr Reuterswärd in response to the opportunity afforded.

Separate Beneficiary process

2 August 2026 - Australian Acoustical Society complaint

Separately from the Trustee's administrative process, the Beneficiary advised the Trustee that, in his private capacity, he had lodged a formal professional-conduct complaint with the Australian Acoustical Society concerning professional acoustic work associated with the Lal Lal Wind Farm.

The Beneficiary provided that information to the Trustee for information and administrative-record purposes only, without requesting that the Trustee adopt or endorse the complaint.

Current status: The existence of the complaint is recorded as a procedural event only. No finding or determination by the Australian Acoustical Society is presently recorded. The Trustee does not presently publish the complaint itself. If the Society later makes findings or otherwise determines the matter, resulting correspondence or a determination may be added to this documentary record where appropriate.
