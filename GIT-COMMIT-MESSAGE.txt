feat(commissioning): add Condition 23 noise sensitive location definition

Update the Final Turbine Commissioning page to make the Planning Permit's
location requirement explicit within the operational-noise pathway.

Add the supplied Planning Permit Condition 23 extract showing that wind farm
sound levels must be determined in accordance with NZS 6808:2010 at noise
sensitive locations "as defined in the Standard".

Add the NZS 6808:2010 definition of Noise sensitive location, including items
(a) to (f), covering residentially zoned land, points within the notional
boundary, habitable residential spaces, educational institutions, licensed
kindergartens and childcare facilities, and temporary accommodation.

Add the associated NZS 6808:2010 Notional boundary definition because the
Noise sensitive location definition expressly includes points within the
notional boundary of buildings containing the specified noise-sensitive spaces.

Explain the documentary consequence that Condition 23 does not leave
"noise sensitive location" as an undefined project-specific expression; the
permit expressly directs the compliance assessment to the NZS 6808:2010
definition.

Cross-reference the NZS 6808, K15aa and Representative Monitoring pages for
the detailed monitoring-location record without making a new technical
compliance finding on the commissioning page.

Preserve all existing commissioning chronology, NCTP, K15aa and reporting
material, update the existing Conditions 23-25 exhibit caption, and update
the commissioning page and sitemap metadata to v8.2.6 dated 9 August 2026.
