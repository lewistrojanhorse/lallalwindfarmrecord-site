feat(k15aa): add permit and NZS governing location pathway

Add Planning Permit Condition 23(a) as the first documentary item on the K15aa
page, recording that wind farm sound levels must be determined in accordance
with NZS 6808:2010 at noise sensitive locations "as defined in the Standard".

Add the relevant NZS Noise sensitive location wording, including its express
reference to points within the notional boundary, together with the NZS
Notional boundary definition of 20 metres from the dwelling or relevant
building, or the legal boundary where closer.

Add the supplied Planning Permit Condition 23 extract as the primary exhibit.

Place the NCTP operational measurement-location requirement immediately after
the governing Permit/NZS pathway, recording that K15aa measurements are to
occur within 20 metres of the dwelling and as close as practicably possible to
the background monitoring location.

Retain and strengthen the accompanying EPA correspondence recording the NZS
notional-boundary definition and the incorporated NCTP pathway under
Regulation 131B.

Renumber the existing documentary record from Items 1-12 to Items 2-13 and
correct the internal "Read with earlier evidence" references following the
insertion.

Preserve the distinction between the governing location framework and the
separate documentary question of how the later K15aa monitoring positions are
reconciled with that framework.

Update K15aa and sitemap metadata to v8.2.7 dated 10 August 2026.
