Regulatory transition

Planning Permit Amendment

The Planning Permit Amendment sought to amend Condition 23 and delete Conditions 24–27 from the Lal Lal Wind Farm planning permit following changes to Victoria's operational wind turbine noise framework. This page records the exhibited proposal, the documentary function of each affected condition, the final permit transition and the Trustee's subsequent administrative clarification record.

Conditions 23–25 in GlossaryRead Framework
Understanding the original framework

Why Conditions 23–27 matter

Conditions 23–27 formed the planning-permit pathway for operational noise limits, the endorsed Noise Compliance Testing Plan, compliance reporting, complaint evaluation and the Noise Complaint Investigation and Response Plan. The exhibited amendment did not propose the same treatment for every condition: it sought to amend Condition 23 and delete Conditions 24–27.

How to read this page

The amendment is part of the same governing pathway

This page does not treat the planning permit amendment as a standalone planning event. It is read against the existing Planning Permit, the endorsed Noise Compliance Testing Plan, the NZS 6808:2010 framework, the Documentary Verification methodology, and the Trustee's crystallised record.

Publicly exhibited scope

What the amendment sought to change

The application report dated 2 October 2025 identified five proposed changes to Planning Permit PL-SP/05/0461-2. The distinction between amendment and deletion is important: Condition 23 was proposed to remain in revised form, while Conditions 24–27 were proposed for deletion.

Regulatory transition

Why the exhibited scope matters

The amendment record is significant because the proposal affected the same permit conditions that created and governed the endorsed NCTP, post-construction assessment, auditor review, compliance publication and complaint-investigation pathways. The final permit transition can therefore be understood only by comparing the exhibited scope with the instrument ultimately issued and the replacement Noise Management Plan framework.

Contemporaneous administrative record

Trustee Formal Submission — 25 February 2026

On 25 February 2026, the Trustee lodged a Formal Submission concerning the proposed amendment to Planning Permit PL-SP:05:0461.

Purpose of the submission

The submission was confined to regulatory sequencing, statutory coherence and orderly transition between the planning-permit framework and the later EPA regulatory framework.

It expressly did not allege non-compliance. It requested confirmation concerning formal discharge of the permit-based framework, the existence and verification of the replacement Noise Management Plan pathway, and whether deletion of Conditions 24–27 should be deferred until those matters were clarified.

Administrative context: The submission forms part of the contemporaneous amendment record and should be read with Annex E, the Noise Management Plan pathway, the NCTP record and the Documentary Verification Schedule.

Transitional Condition 27A

Administrative clarification and reconciliation record

The final amended permit was issued after exhibition of a proposal to amend Condition 23 and delete Conditions 24–27. Comparison of the final instrument with the exhibited material identified additional transitional wording and other differences requiring administrative clarification, including Transitional Condition 27A, responsible-authority wording and meteorological monitoring facility wording.

The correspondence was confined to administrative clarification and record purposes. It did not seek enforcement action, review of the Department's decision or another administrative outcome.

27A

Transitional framework

The final permit required the endorsed NCTP and Noise Complaint Investigation and Response Plan to be maintained until a regulation 131E Noise Management Plan had been prepared and implemented.

RA

Responsible authority

The Trustee sought clarification concerning differences between the publicly exhibited responsible-authority wording and the wording in the final permit instrument.

MM

Meteorological monitoring

The Trustee also sought clarification concerning changes to wording for the permanent meteorological monitoring facilities and whether those changes formed part of the exhibited scope.

Evidence before opinion

The page records the transition question, not a finding

Verification question: Does the disclosed documentary record enable readers to verify how the proposed amendment affected the governing planning permit framework and the relationship between the original permit conditions and the later EPA regulatory framework?

The record does not determine whether the amendment should or should not be approved. It records that the proposed removal or alteration of permit-based operational noise conditions must be read alongside the existing documentary record concerning those same conditions, the NCTP pathway, post-construction assessment, auditor material, agency custody and FOI disclosures.

Evidentiary limit: This page records the documentary and administrative significance of the amendment pathway. It does not substitute for a regulator, decision-maker, tribunal or court.
